# SoundGuard Layer 3 participant information

Study version: **L3_PROTOCOL_V1**

## Purpose and invitation

You are invited to help evaluate SoundGuard, a research prototype intended to
present live speech subtitles and visual notices for environmental and safety
sounds. The study asks whether this additional visual aid benefits people with
hearing impairment. SoundGuard is a prototype, not a certified medical or
safety device, and must not be relied on for real emergencies.

## What participation involves

The session takes approximately **25–40 minutes**. You will complete the same
four kinds of tasks both without SoundGuard assistance and with SoundGuard
assistance: understanding short Vietnamese speech, identifying environmental
sounds, responding to overlapping speech and environmental sounds, and
recognizing simulated safety/HELP events. You will answer multiple-choice
questions and then complete the standard 10-item System Usability Scale and
seven SoundGuard-specific 1–5 rating items.

All safety sounds are prerecorded or scripted simulations. There will be no
real dangerous event. You may keep using your normal hearing aid, cochlear
implant, or other usual assistance, but its use should stay the same in both
conditions.

## Online speech recognition and privacy

SoundGuard’s current speech-to-text function uses **Google Speech
Recognition**. Audio processed for live subtitles may be transmitted to an
external online speech-recognition service, so stable internet connectivity is
required for speech and mixed trials. Please do not intentionally say any
sensitive or private information. Only the supplied study-relevant speech
stimuli should be used. We make no claim here about whether an external service
provider stores or does not store audio.

The local research dataset uses an anonymous code such as P01 and must not
contain your name. It records task responses, correctness, timing, observed
SoundGuard display state, technical failures, optional broad demographic
categories, and questionnaire answers. The researcher test used by preflight
is not participant data.

## Choice and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may pause, skip, or stop at any time without
penalty and without giving a reason. Ask the researcher any questions before
signing the consent form. Stopping collection does not automatically determine
whether already collected anonymous data can be withdrawn; the researcher must
explain the locally approved withdrawal procedure before the session.

No ethics approval is asserted by this document. The research team must obtain
and document every approval required by its school, competition, institution,
and local law before recruitment.
