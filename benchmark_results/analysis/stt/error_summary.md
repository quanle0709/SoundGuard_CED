# STT error summary

All 100 clean VIVOS utterances were analyzed. Corpus edit counts: 88 substitutions, 21 deletions, 2 insertions over 1033 reference words. Highest-WER utterances are listed first in `error_analysis.csv`.

Mean per-utterance CER change after stripping Vietnamese combining marks from both reference and hypothesis: 0.0093. This is a diagnostic sensitivity estimate, not a claim that accents should be ignored. Mean WER for <=7-word utterances: 0.1357; for longer utterances: 0.1045. Speaker mean-WER range: 0.0000 to 0.3906. The small per-speaker sample counts preclude a demographic conclusion.
